# Operation Front Door — a Claim Checker audit, presented as a StoryTeller story

A worked example of [Claim Checker](https://claimchecker.teambotics.app), the open
method for checking public claims with an AI agent, run by TeamBotics on the
public record of **Paradox's "Operation Front Door"** deployment claims — and
presented the way every Claim Checker audit is presented: as a
[StoryTeller](https://storyteller.teambotics.app/) story.

**Read it:** open [`index.html`](index.html) — a self-contained story graph of
the audit: 40 nodes, 61 edges, and 5 guided paths ending on the Next Questions
the audit earned. The underlying test record: 247 exchanges across 32
deployments — 3 PASS · 42 PARTIAL · 202 FAIL under the published rubric.

**The record behind the story:** the published Operation Front Door record at
[frontdoor.teambotics.app](https://frontdoor.teambotics.app) — the findings
page, the evidence vault, the customer list, and the Concierge page. The story
is built from that published record only. Its framing, held throughout:
collection without custody — the audit makes no claim of illegality or theft.

- `research.md` — the story's own research record, including its known gaps.
- `story.json` and `theme.json` — the story's source files.
- Validator: 0 errors / 0 warnings.

The method: [Claim Checker](https://github.com/Teambotics-Inc/claim-checker)
(Standard v1.0). The presentation companion:
[StoryTeller](https://storyteller.teambotics.app/).

*An independent StoryTeller story by Claim Checker / TeamBotics. Paradox did
not commission, review, or endorse this audit.*
